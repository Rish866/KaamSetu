# KaamSetu Privacy Notice — Draft for Legal Review

KaamSetu is designed to collect only the information needed to match candidates and verified employers. Candidate contact details and resumes are not shown in employer discovery results. They are shared only when a candidate applies or accepts an invitation.

Users can turn employer discovery off, withdraw optional consent, request a copy of their data and request account deletion. Marketing consent is separate from essential account and application notifications.

The production service will identify its operating legal entity, privacy contact, processors, retention periods and grievance route before accepting real personal data. This draft must be reviewed by qualified Indian counsel before public launch.
