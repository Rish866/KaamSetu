## Full description
KaamSetu helps job seekers in Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane and Pune find work with verified employers, and helps
employers hire without guesswork.

For candidates
• Every job shows its salary range.
• Every employer is verified by our trust team before posting.
• Each job has a match score with plain-language reasons: skills, location, salary, experience and freshness.
• Your phone number and email stay private until you are shortlisted and choose to share them.
• Track every application: viewed, shortlisted, interview, offer.
• Confirm interviews or ask for another time in one tap.
• Download your data or delete your account at any time.

For employers
• Post salary-visible jobs after a quick company verification.
• See applicants ranked by an explainable score.
• Move candidates through a clear pipeline and schedule interviews.
• Invite candidates who have chosen to be discoverable.
• Add recruiters and viewers to your team.

KaamSetu never asks candidates to pay for jobs. Report any job that does.
