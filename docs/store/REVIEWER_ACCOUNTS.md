# Test accounts for store reviewers and smoke tests

Create these in **production** before submission (owner or admin). They must be clearly fictional and contain no real person's data.
Store the passwords only in the owner's password manager and paste them directly into the Play Console / App Store Connect "App access" fields.

| Account | How to create | Notes for reviewers |
|---------|---------------|---------------------|
| reviewer.candidate@getkaamsetu.in | Sign up as Candidate in the app | Profile pre-filled with sample skills; discovery on |
| reviewer.employer@getkaamsetu.in | Sign up as Employer, create company "KaamSetu Review Co" | Admin approves the company with reason "App review test company"; one published job "Test Role (App Review)" with a clear salary |

Reviewer note text: "Sign in with the candidate account to browse jobs and apply. Sign in with the employer account to see the
applicant and move them through the pipeline. Contact details stay hidden until the employer shortlists and the candidate consents
in Settings. No payment or SMS is required."

Mark the test company and job with "(App Review)" so real candidates are not misled, and pause the job after review.
